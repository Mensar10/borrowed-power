Amount:

The terminal puts the amount inside its question to the card. The card answers with a one-time code that depends on that amount.

Same carrier, different message. The 13.56 MHz wave and the power to the card stay the same. Only the pattern of bits changes.

The message (bank-card mode)Carrier: 13.56 MHz · Power to card: same
Reader → card (question with the amount)
Card → reader (one-time code)

Above a set limit, the terminal asks for your PIN.

The question we started with

Where does the chip get its power, and how does the card answer without a transmitter?

Try: answer it in your head first, then check.

The reader's changing magnetic field induces a current in the card's coil, and this powers the chip. The card answers by switching a load on and off, so it takes a little more power, then a little less. The reader feels these dips.
